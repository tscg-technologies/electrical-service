FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

General Electrical Enquiries

Common questions regarding electrical estimates, safety clearances, and property preparations.

Typically, contractors review property square footage, existing main service capacity (single-phase vs three-phase), switchboard age, and the specific load ratings of new appliances or EV chargers being installed.

Breaker trips are safety mechanisms designed to prevent wire overheating and electrical fire. Trips are commonly caused by circuit overload (too many high-draw devices on one branch), direct short circuits, or ground fault earth leakage.

Most homes can accommodate Level 2 charging, but a licensed professional must assess whether the existing incoming feed and distribution board can carry 32A or 40A of continuous load without requiring a panel upgrade or load-shedding controller.

Residential properties should undergo a comprehensive periodic safety inspection every 3 to 5 years, or prior to purchasing/selling. Commercial spaces and rental properties typically require more frequent compliance audits depending on local building codes.
